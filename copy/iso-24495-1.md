# FAQ (Faq)

## FAQ
- Section eyebrow: Help
- Section headline: Quick answers.
- Section subhead: The stuff people ask before they install.

- Q: Is it legal and safe?
  - A: Yes. AnimeTV doesn't host, upload, or store any video. It just finds public streams from other sites for you. Use a VPN if your country blocks those sites.

- Q: How do I sync my AniList or MAL?
  - A: Open the app, go to Settings, pick Trackers, and tap Connect. Sign in once. Your progress updates from there.

- Q: Will it run on my TV box or Firestick?
  - A: Yes. Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV all work. Download the APK with the "Downloader" app and install it.

- Q: Can I change subtitles or use a different video player?
  - A: Yes. You can load .SRT or .VTT subtitle files, switch audio tracks, and pick an external player from the settings.
