AnimeTV — Free Anime App for Android TV, Firestick & Windows (No Ads)

Watch 1080p anime free on Android phones, Android TV, Amazon Firestick, and Windows 10/11. 100% ad-free, open-source under GPLv3, with zero account registration needed.

Key Features

  • Zero Advertisements & No Tracking: Pure anime streaming without popups, video ads, or telemetry.
  • Built for TV Remote D-Pads: Seamless couch navigation on Google TV, Fire TV, and Android TV boxes.
  • Multi-Source Scrapers: Stream in 1080p and 4K from Miruro, Animepahe, Anikoto, and AnimeFlix mirrors.
  • Automatic Tracker Sync: Real-time scrobbling and watchlist tracking with AniList, MyAnimeList (MAL), and Trakt.
  • DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH): Built-in DNS routing to bypass ISP domain blocks and streaming throttling.
  • Cross-Platform Support: Available on Android APK, Fire OS, Windows x64, and Windows ARM64.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AnimeTV legal and safe to use?
Yes. AnimeTV does not host or store videos; it aggregates public streams. For private streaming, a VPN is recommended.
How do I sync AniList or MyAnimeList?
Go to Settings > Trackers > Connect in the app and sign in once to sync your watch progress.
Will AnimeTV work on Firestick and Android TV?
Yes, sideload the APK using the Downloader app on Fire TV or install directly on Android TV.
Can I load external subtitles and players?
Yes, .SRT/.VTT subtitles, audio track switching (Sub/Dub), and external players (VLC, MPV) are supported.
